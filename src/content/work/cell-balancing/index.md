## Overview

Active cell balancing moves charge between the cells of a battery pack. This project applies **boundary (sliding-mode) control** to inductor-based balancing of Li-ion cells.

## What I did

- Derived **closed-form elliptical switching surfaces** for inductor-based active cell balancing.
- Extended them to a **real OCV model** using a logarithmic energy function.
- Covered **2-cell and 4-cell** topologies, modelled in MATLAB/Simulink.
